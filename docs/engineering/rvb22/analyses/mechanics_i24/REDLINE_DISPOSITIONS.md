# I24 mechanical redline disposition

The frozen C06/W03/T04 source is an authored rework candidate; **mechanical acceptance remains open**. C05/W02/T03 is the prior rework baseline with acceptance withdrawn in I23. There is no accepted mechanical configuration.

Changes after the rejected first C06 trial: the cap edge retreats 0.4 mm; long free beam elements are subdivided to 0.1 mm; minimum-thickness/modulus inertia bounds replace maximum-thickness-only displacement; complete moments and stress demand are exported; the central shoe becomes 12.8 mm and the cold assembly moves 3 mm deeper; the upper carrier window reaches Y=+0.7 mm; independent ear-edge keys and proposed M2 preload controls are added; whole-body locating motion, robust root-bearing contact and complete carrier/service sweeps are evaluated; the full moving 851 cable conflict is removed with a local post and supported root-band relief. The immediately preceding full-depth narrow-band relief trial exceeded the force-only allocation and was rejected before the outer band was shortened. The original 100-leaf nominal construction remains a trial, not a qualified improvement over the 200-leaf baseline.
