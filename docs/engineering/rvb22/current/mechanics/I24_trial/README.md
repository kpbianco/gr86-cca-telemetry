## Manufacturing and assembly conditions

1. Machine the insulating carrier, support/capture parts and locator keys to the SCAD source geometry. Specify the required hot flexural, compression and modulus properties at the actual analyzed material temperature. Material selection, creep and retained dimensions are still open; a room-temperature catalogue value is insufficient.
2. Keep the 100 leaves individually free throughout each entire flex region. Weld or braze only the defined hot and distal cold packets, before tape installation. Demonstrate that heat-affected zones, solder/braze wicking and clamping do not shorten the free span or reduce the required leaf strength. Individual forming paths and fatigue qualification remain open.
3. Retain 0.127 mm insulating thermal tape at the three PCB contacts. The T04 shoe and its bonded packet must be assembled before PCB attachment. Include the additional shoe lever moment and peel force in the qualified hot-joint process.
4. Install the PCB within the four separate ear-edge locating features, then fit the removable capture caps and insulating clearance sleeves. Verify insertion clearance, the 1.85–1.95 mm effective capture gap, root-band contact and the complete PCB-to-carrier rigid-body envelope before process acceptance.
5. The proposed M2 installation window is 0.02–0.04 N·m, conditional on a verified nut factor of 0.15–0.30. Its estimated preload is 33.3–133.3 N. The sleeve calculation includes this preload plus the service load against a 100 MPa hot compression requirement. Actual hardware/friction, thread retention, creep, re-torque rules and thermal-cycle capture dimensions remain unqualified; this is not a validated production torque specification.
6. Support each full distal landing against the defined cold structure. Keep the free straight spans physically open; neither adhesive, a broad backing plate nor a clamp may touch them. M3 hardware dimensions and whole-footprint heat-sink contact must meet the authored envelope and thermal conditions.
7. Preserve the selected 150±3 mm Adafruit 851 route, 2.0 mm maximum OD envelope, 15 mm minimum bend radius, negative-Y U.FL exit and independent cable restraint. The separate 60°C accessory applicability issue remains unresolved. Install and service the miniature connector with the PCB removed.
